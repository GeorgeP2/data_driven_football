Chance of winning the league

Who moved the needle

Week-on-week change in win chance. Gains stack above zero, losses below. Changes smaller than the simulation noise (Welch t-test, p > 0.05) are set to zero, so every bar is a real shift.

Points behind the leader

Distance to whoever was top that week. Zero is the leader.

Gameweek scores

Points each week before transfer hits, as FPL shows them. Hits come off the season total.

Where everyone finishes

Share of 10,000 simulated seasons that end with each manager in each position. This is a separate run from the win-chance chart, so first-place figures can differ from it by about a point.

How the numbers are made

Each manager's weekly rate is their running average, pulled toward the league average with a prior worth a few gameweeks of evidence, and drawn each simulation with its standard error. Weekly scores are drawn from a normal distribution with a spread of 16 points, which is last season's within-manager variation. A Poisson model would give a spread of about 7.5 at 55 points and call early leads near-certain.

The form-adjusted chance in the table runs the same simulation on the exponentially weighted recent average instead of the season average. Where the two disagree, a manager's level has changed and the season average has not caught up.
